SDLPoP alternate graphics palace-wall renderer fix
===================================================

Problem:
In Palace levels under VGA, the original DOS renderer suppresses the normal
wall base/main sprites and procedurally paints DOS palace brick faces. It then
uses selected RSET_WALL frames as tiny divider decals.

The SNES, SNES Alt and Mac Prince packs use the normal wall sprite-table
semantics instead: frames 3/4, 5/6, 7/8 and 9/10 are complete wall base/main
pairs. Feeding those packs through the DOS-only palace path causes full-height
wall faces to be drawn as tiny decals, producing the large corrupt rectangles.

Fix:
- Keep the original DOS procedural palace path only when graphics_pack == dos.
- For alternate graphics packs, allow the normal wall bottom/main sprites to
  render in Palace VGA mode.
- Route alternate packs through the generic wall-decoration path.
- Do not modify any .pak assets.

Modified file:
  src/seg008.c

This supersedes the earlier asset hotfix that replaced VPALACE 360-377 with
DOS resources. Use the ORIGINAL res_snes.pak, res_snes_alt.pak and
res_mac_prince.pak included in this archive.
